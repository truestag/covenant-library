# Covenant Library v1.0.4 - Text Integrity Repair

## Scope

This candidate repairs three user-reported reader/search defects without changing the accepted v1.0.2 or v1.0.3 builds:

- Jerusalem Talmud - Shabbat (Heinrich W. Guggenheimer English translation)
- The Apocalypse of Abraham
- The Ascension of Isaiah

## Shabbat / Guggenheimer repair

The legacy extraction flattened Sefaria footnote markers into adjacent text. The v1.0.4 second-pass repair restores explicit marker boundaries while preserving the complete source wording. Examples include:

- `HALAKHAH: 25For` -> `HALAKHAH: [25] For`
- `written1972K. 19:3` -> `written [197] 2K. 19:3`
- `riders2302K. 2:12` -> `riders [230] 2K. 2:12`
- `hands2191S. 8:4` -> `hands [219] 1S. 8:4`
- `Eterna1153Num. 31:50` -> `Eternal [153] Num. 31:50`

Legitimate folio and tractate references such as `Babli 80b` remain untouched.

The normalization was run across all 39 Guggenheimer Yerushalmi tractates. The v1.0.4 pass normalized 2,546 additional malformed boundaries in 2,143 segments; the resulting corpus contains 50,109 explicit Guggenheimer footnote markers.

## Apocalypse of Abraham

The prior local JSON was a chapter-level machine extraction from the 1919 SPCK scan. Printed notes and page material had leaked into the reading text.

v1.0.4 replaces it with a clean reading transcription structured as:

- 32 chapters
- 295 verse segments
- no printed page headers, footnote blocks, or editorial page furniture in reader text

The transcription is based on the pinned historical reading source and checked against the public-domain 1919 SPCK witness already held in the Covenant Library source archive. This is a reading transcription, not a replacement for critical apparatus.

## Ascension of Isaiah

The prior local JSON had the same scan-extraction contamination. v1.0.4 replaces it with a clean verse-level reading transcription:

- 11 chapters
- 297 segments, including the chapter 6 heading segment
- restored separate 4:8 and 8:18 boundaries
- corrected demonstrable OCR/transcription errors against the 1919 witness, including the malformed `th3e Spirit` reading
- removed printed notes and page furniture from reader text

As with Apocalypse of Abraham, this is a clean public-domain reading transcription; critical notes remain a separate research concern.

## Validation

The v1.0.4 corpus validates with:

- 71 collections
- 1,487 catalog records
- 960 locally readable works
- 18,555 sections
- 414,229 searchable segments
- 64 search shards

Regression tests cover the repaired Shabbat marker forms, preserved legitimate citations, Yoma's previously repaired markers, Apocalypse of Abraham verse structure, Ascension of Isaiah verse structure, recovered verse boundaries, and absence of scan/page-furniture contamination.

A final Windows runtime smoke test confirmed that the packaged v1.0.4 application serves the corrected embedded Apocalypse of Abraham, Ascension of Isaiah, Shabbat, and Yoma data.

## Release handling

v1.0.4 is preserved as a candidate for PC verification. The accepted v1.0.2 and prior v1.0.3 candidate remain unchanged.
