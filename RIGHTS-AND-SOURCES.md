# Rights, Sources, and Attribution

Covenant Library contains material from many independent sources and rights regimes. There is **no blanket license for all textual content in this repository**. Each work/dataset retains its own rights and provenance metadata. A source link does not by itself imply redistribution permission.

## General corpus policy

- Public-domain historical texts may be embedded when their source identity is preserved.
- Licensed texts/data are distributed only under their applicable license and attribution conditions.
- Works without a resolved redistribution basis remain source-link-only or acquisition-pending.
- Modern editorial additions are not treated as public domain merely because the underlying historical work is public domain.
- Distinct translations and textual witnesses are not silently fused.

The reader's catalog/source-index metadata is the work-level source of truth for provenance and redistribution status.
